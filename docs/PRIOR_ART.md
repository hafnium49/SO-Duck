# Related open-source work (public search, 22 Nov 2026)

- [Open Duck Mini V2](https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini/tree/v2): open biped mechanics, 10 leg + 4 neck actuators, source CAD and hardware instructions; does **not** include this neck-to-wrist SO-101 gripper conversion.
- [SO-101](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100): low-cost servo arm with flex/roll wrist and jaw; source donor parts.
- [Yo.Town Gym](https://github.com/yotown/gym): source-level parametric CAD for **both** Duck and SO-101 as separate robot families; includes a **Flamingo** Duck variant with longer neck/legs and wings. Flamingo does **not** document the six-pose-DoF manipulator proposed here. Its published marching study cautions about knee torque exceeding nominal continuous ratings.
- [matte1782/robot_jarvis](https://github.com/matte1782/robot_jarvis): text design proposal for torso-mounted 2-DoF arms on Duck, not a publicly verified complete physical assembly. Some descriptions mistakenly use conventional PWM architecture for the Duck's serial-bus servos.
- [ToddlerBot](https://github.com/hshi74/toddlerbot): general whole-body locomotion and grasping precedent, substantially different physical scale and kinematics.
- [LeRobot humanoid design](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot-humanoid-design): motor and arm configuration optimization references.

**Claim scope:** this search did not confirm a physical Duck V2 + SO-101 neck-gripper fusion. This is *not* a novelty or patent clearance claim. Add new competing implementations with specific source revisions and evidence level (CAD, simulator, physical video, released BOM).
